Our Core Services

From software and digital products to on-site infrastructure, digitalcoretech helps you plan, implement, and maintain technology that supports daily operations.

  • Management System Development

    We analyze your workflows and develop secure, role-based management systems for records, scheduling, inventory, approvals, and reporting. Solutions are tailored to your processes and designed to grow with your organization.

  • Website Development

    We design and build responsive websites with clear navigation, accessible layouts, fast-loading pages, and content structured around your services. From a company profile to a custom web platform, the result is easy to use on mobile and desktop.

  • Mobile App Development

    We create mobile applications for iOS and Android that make your services accessible on the go. Work includes product planning, interface design, app development, testing, deployment guidance, and ongoing improvements.

  • CCTV Camera Installation

    We assess your site, recommend suitable camera coverage, install and configure CCTV equipment, and set up recording and authorized remote viewing. We also provide handover guidance and maintenance options for dependable monitoring.

  • Software Repair

    We diagnose software faults, resolve installation and configuration issues, remove unwanted or conflicting applications, and apply updates. Where possible, we help restore normal operation while taking care to protect business data.

  • Networking

    We plan, install, and troubleshoot wired and wireless networks, including routers, switches, Wi-Fi coverage, and shared resources. Our goal is stable connectivity with sensible access controls and room for future expansion.

  • Security

    We help strengthen digital and physical security through access-control planning, device and network hardening, CCTV integration, updates, and practical risk-reduction guidance tailored to your environment.

  • Technical Support 24/7

    Our 24/7 support channel helps you report urgent technical issues and request troubleshooting for systems, software, devices, and connectivity. Support scope and response arrangements are agreed to suit your needs.
